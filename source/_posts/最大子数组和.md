## 最大子数组和

| `-2` | `1`  | `-3` | `4`  | `-1` | `2`  | `1`  | `-5` | `4`  |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| -2   | 1    | 1    | 4    | 4    | 5    | 6    | 6    | 6    |

> 思路：
>
> 计算加入的新值和有没有之前的值大，然后更新最大值

```Python
def maxSum(nums):
    curr = maxSum = nums[0]
    for i in range(1,len(nums)):
        curr = max(nums[i]+curr,nums[i])
        maxSum = max(curr,maxSum)
    return maxSum
```
